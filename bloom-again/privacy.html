Privacy Policy

Bloom Again: Merge Story · Effective November 27, 2026

The short version: we don't ask for your name, email, contacts or location, and your game progress stays on your device. To show ads and sell in-game items, the game uses services from Google and Unity. Those services collect some device data, such as your advertising ID, as described below.

This policy explains what information the mobile game Bloom Again: Merge Story (the "Game") collects and why. The Game is made by Clevy Labs ("we", "us"). If you have any question, email us at clevylabs@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not run our own servers or accounts for the Game. We don't collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device. That includes your board, coins, gems, energy, zones and language. We can't see this data. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes it.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and keep it only as long as needed to help you.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The Game includes the software kits (SDKs) below. Each one collects data under its own privacy policy. We don't sell your personal information.

3. Ads and your choices

The Game shows ads, including rewarded videos that you choose to watch for in-game rewards. Ads may be personalized (based on your advertising ID and interests) or non-personalized.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the Game under Settings → Privacy options.
  • On any Android device, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Buying the No Ads pack turns off ads that appear between play sessions. Rewarded videos stay available if you want them.

4. In-app purchases

The Game is free to play and offers optional in-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive only confirmation of what was bought, so the Game can deliver it.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Game data on your device: kept until you uninstall the Game or clear its data.
  • Emails you send us: kept only as long as needed to answer you. Ask us and we'll delete them.
  • Data held by Google or Unity: kept according to their policies. You can manage Google data at myaccount.google.com.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to or limit how it's used. We hold almost no personal data about you ourselves. You can still email us with any request and we'll respond within 30 days. For data held by Google or Unity, we'll also point you to the right place.

8. Security

Data sent by the SDKs above is transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transfer is 100% secure, but we choose established providers and keep the data we handle to a minimum.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above. If a change is significant, we'll also mention it in the Game or in the store listing.
