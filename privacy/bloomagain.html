Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Clevy Labs built Bloom Again: Merge Story as a free-to-play mobile game. This page informs users about our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when using the game.

Information Collection and Use

Bloom Again: Merge Story does not require users to create an account or provide personal information directly.

Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device. We do not receive them, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

The game may use third-party services that collect information used to identify your device or improve their services.

Third-Party Services

The game may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
  • Google User Messaging Platform (Consent)
  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases)
  • Unity (Game Engine and In-App Purchasing)

These services may collect information such as:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Usage and diagnostic information
  • IP address
  • Purchase history

For more information about how these services handle user data, please review:

  • Google's Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Unity's Privacy Policy

Advertisements

Bloom Again: Merge Story may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners, including optional rewarded videos. These partners may use device identifiers and similar technologies to provide and measure advertisements.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy Options. On any Android device, you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings.

In-App Purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases, such as gem packs and removing advertisements. Payments are processed securely through Google Play and are subject to Google's terms and policies. We do not see or store your payment details.

Deleting Your Data

Bloom Again: Merge Story has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. You can remove your data in these ways:

  • Game progress and settings: these are stored only on your device. Uninstall the game, or go to your device's Settings → Apps → Bloom Again → Storage → Clear data, and they are deleted immediately.
  • Advertising data: on Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and delete or reset your advertising ID. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you can also withdraw ad consent in the game under Settings → Privacy Options.
  • Anything else, such as emails you sent us: email clevylabs@gmail.com with the subject "Bloom Again data deletion request". We will delete it and confirm within 30 days.

Purchase records are kept by Google Play under its own policies and can be managed in your Google account at myaccount.google.com.

Data Security

We value your trust and strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect information related to the use of our game. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

Bloom Again: Merge Story does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
